Which watch to buy for running in 2026. What really matters, where you can save, and which model fits what you need.

First, the main thing

A watch won't make you faster. Training will. You need a watch to:

measure pace and distance accurately
hold the right heart rate or pace in a workout
get workouts from your coach straight to your wrist
see your fitness grow

So don't choose by the number of features. Choose by four things.

4 things that matter

1
Accurate GPS. Look for dual-band GPS (L1+L5). In the city, near tall buildings and in the woods your pace won't jump around, and the distance will match the real one.
2
Workouts on the watch. Intervals come from your coach or an app, and the watch guides you through each segment: how long to run and at what pace. Check that the watch works with TrainingPeaks.
3
Battery. 20 hours or more with GPS is enough for a week of training and a marathon without charging.
4
Comfort. Light, under 50 g. A bright screen. Buttons, not only a touchscreen: in the rain and with gloves on, touch lets you down.

Don't overpay for

maps, if you run familiar routes
titanium, sapphire and solar charging
dozens of "smart" metrics: stress, "body age" and so on
ECG, flashlight, LTE
music, if you run with your phone or without music

Put the money you save into good running shoes or a chest heart rate strap.

Just starting out

Garmin Forerunner 70

Bright AMOLED screen and all of Garmin's training features. The best way into the Garmin world. No contactless payments and no dual-band GPS.

Coros Pace 4

Dual-band GPS, up to 31 hours with GPS, only 32 g, offline maps. No payments and no music. The best accuracy for the money.

Huawei Watch GT Runner 2

34.5 g, up to 32 hours with dual-band GPS. The downside: it doesn't work with TrainingPeaks directly. You can send workouts to it through Intervals.icu.

Garmin Forerunner 165

The previous generation, but it has everything you need for running. A good option if you find it on sale for less than the 70.

Running regularly, training for races

Garmin Forerunner 170

Everything the 70 has, plus contactless payments and a barometer. There's also a Music version.

Suunto Run 2

35 g, dual-band GPS, bright screen, free offline maps.

Polar Vantage M3

Dual-band GPS, AMOLED, strong analysis of training load and recovery.

Amazfit Cheetah 2 Pro

Maps, titanium, payments, works with TrainingPeaks. In dense city areas its GPS is weaker than the competition.

Serious training, triathlon

Garmin Forerunner 570

my pick

My pick for most runners. Dual-band GPS, bright screen, all of Garmin's training metrics. No full maps.

Garmin Forerunner 970

Same, plus maps, a bigger battery, a flashlight and ECG. If you want maps and everything else.

Coros Pace 4 Pro

New in September 2026: a very bright screen, maps, titanium and sapphire. Similar features, but cheaper than Garmin.

Trail and ultra

Coros Apex 4

A mountain watch with offline maps and a big battery. The best price in its class.

Garmin Enduro 4

New in October 2026. Solar charging and enough battery for multi-day races.

Garmin Fenix 9

Maps, flashlight, tough case. For people who want one watch for everything. More than you need for road running.

What about Apple Watch?

You can run with it, and the GPS is good. But you'll charge it every 1-2 days, workouts from a coach only arrive through third-party apps, and the touchscreen is awkward in the rain and with gloves.

If you already have one, start with it. If you're buying a watch for running, get a sports watch.

Do you need a chest strap?

The wrist sensor works well on steady easy runs. It gets it wrong on intervals, in the cold and with tattoos on the wrist.

If you train by heart rate, a chest strap gives you accurate numbers. Any strap with Bluetooth and ANT+ works, for example the Polar H10.

If you're new, you can start without one and buy it later.

In short: what to pick

just startingGarmin Forerunner 70 or Coros Pace 4
training for racesGarmin Forerunner 170 or Suunto Run 2
the most for runningGarmin Forerunner 570
trail and ultraCoros Apex 4

Before you buy, compare prices from a few sellers and check the warranty.
